Dear Baby Jaden 💙

A special letter from Tita Yssa & Nanay Ebeng
"Children are a gift from the Lord; they are a reward from him."

— Psalm 127:3

Dear Baby Jaden,

Even though we didn't get to see you lagi, lagi ka lang namin nakikita sa video call, we want you to know that we love you so much. 💙

Every time we see your cute little face on our video calls, you always bring happiness to us. Kahit screen lang ang pagitan natin, you are always close to our hearts.

Sana paglaki mo, maging mabait ka, mapagmahal, magalang, at may mabuting puso. Be kind to everyone and always remember to respect and love the people around you.

Sana maging katulad ka ng Papa mo — mabait, mapagmahal, at marunong rumespeto. We hope that you will grow up to be a good and loving person.

We pray that God will always protect you, guide you, and bless you as you grow. May He give you a happy childhood, a healthy life, and a heart full of love.

Always remember, Baby Jaden, that even though we may not always be beside you, we will always love you, pray for you, and cheer for you.

We can't wait to see all the wonderful things you will become someday. Grow up happy, kind, loving, and faithful.

With all our love, 💙

Tita Yssa
&
Nanay Ebeng
